About Yorkshire Dales Handyman

Yorkshire Dales Handyman provides practical, dependable help for the jobs around your home, garden and property that need doing properly.

Whether it is a repair that has been waiting too long, decorating and minor finishing work, a garden that needs bringing back under control, or furniture and storage that needs assembling, I take a straightforward and careful approach. I turn up when agreed, work tidily and communicate clearly.

Before work begins, we will agree what needs doing and the price for the scope of work. If anything unexpected comes up, I will explain it and agree any changes with you before carrying on.

I cover the Aire Valley, Wharfedale and Craven, and I am happy to help homeowners, landlords, holiday-let owners and second-home owners who need a trusted local person for one-off jobs or ongoing maintenance.

I do not undertake gas work, major electrical work, structural alterations, high-level tree work or specialist work requiring certification. If a job needs a specialist, I will tell you honestly before any work begins.
