About Yorkshire Dales Handyman

I set up Yorkshire Dales Handyman because homeowners across the Dales deserve better than a clock-watching odd-jobber or a three-week wait for a tradesperson who won't take small jobs.

I work one fixed price at a time — agreed with you in writing before I start, so there's never a surprise on the invoice. Whether it's bespoke alcove shelving in a period cottage, a luxury tap swap, or finishing touches on a second home you only visit at weekends, I treat every property with the same care I'd want in my own home: dust sheets down, everything protected, and the space left cleaner than I found it.

I'm fully insured, punctual, and happy to hold keys for second-home owners who need a trusted pair of hands nearby. If a job needs a certified gas or electrical specialist, I'll say so up front and point you to someone properly qualified — I only take on the work I can guarantee to a premium standard.

Covering Ingleton, Settle, Kirkby Lonsdale, Sedbergh and the surrounding Three Shires border, with regular visits into Wharfedale and beyond — if you're not sure whether you're in range, just ask.
