About Us

Yorkshire Dales Handyman provides practical, dependable help for the jobs around your home, garden and property. I turn up when agreed, work tidily and agree the price before any work begins.

I cover the Aire Valley, Wharfedale and Craven for homeowners, landlords and holiday let owners. I do not do gas, major electrical, structural or high level tree work, and I will tell you honestly if a job needs a specialist.

Common Questions

Which areas do you cover?

The Aire Valley, Wharfedale and Craven. Not sure if you are in range? Send your postcode and we will let you know.

How does pricing work?

Every job is priced for the work involved. We agree the price before work starts, and talk to you first about materials or anything extra.

Are you insured?

Yes, with £2m public liability cover.

What jobs do you not do?

Gas work, major electrical work, structural alterations, high level tree work and anything needing specialist certification. If a job needs a specialist, we will say so before any work begins.

My job is not on the list. Can you help?

Get in touch. If it is within our scope, we will be happy to quote.
